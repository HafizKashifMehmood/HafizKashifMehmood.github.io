Faculty Profile

Qazi Muhammad Kashif Mehmood

Known to students as "Hafiz Kashif" · Senior Faculty, Quantitative Analysis for Business (Quantitative Methods) & PRC-2

A dedicated and highly respected faculty member, recognized for turning intimidating numbers into confident, exam-ready skills.

Senior Faculty at SKANS School of Accountancy, Multan Campus, teaching Quantitative Analysis for Business (Quantitative Methods) and the PRC-2 paper to CA aspirants for the past 16 years.

A firm believer in learning by doing — the teaching approach centers on worked examples and repeated practice, breaking every formula down to why it works, not just how to use it. The result: students who can reason through a problem in the exam hall, not just recall a memorized script.

Known among students for patience, clarity, and an ability to make even the most complex statistical concepts feel approachable — building strong, industry-ready foundational quantitative skills that carry well beyond the classroom.

For the past 26 years, creating impact across students' lives through the practical, real-world teaching of mathematics and statistics.
