Qazi Muhammad Kashif Mehmood

Known to students as "Hafiz Kashif" · Senior Faculty, Quantitative Methods (QM) & PRC-2

Hafiz Kashif has spent his career helping accountancy students build genuine confidence with numbers — not just for exams, but as a working skill for business decisions. At SKANS School of Accountancy, Multan Campus, he teaches Quantitative Methods and PRC-2 (Quantitative Analysis for Business), guiding students from foundational probability and statistics through to the applied techniques examined at the professional level.

His teaching style favours worked examples and repeated practice: breaking every formula down to why it works, not just how to use it, so that students walk into the exam hall able to reason through a problem rather than recall a script.
